# super-market-grocery-sales
Supermarket Grocery Sales Dashboard

Student: Pranav Roy
Tool Used: Microsoft Excel
Project Type: Retail Sales Data Analytics

1. Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing supermarket grocery sales data to understand business performance, sales trends, profitability, and customer purchasing patterns.
The dashboard helps management identify high-performing product categories, regions, and cities, while also understanding the relationship between discounts and profit.

2. Dataset
Dataset Name: Supermart Grocery Sales – Retail Analytics
Dataset Information:
- Total Records / Orders: 9,994 (as displayed in the dashboard)
- Data Period: 2015–2018
- Data Type: Retail Sales Data

Important Columns:
- Category
- Sub-Category
- City
- Order Date
- Region
- Sales
- Discount
- Profit
- State
- Year

3. Data Preparation
The following data-cleaning and preparation activities were performed in Excel:
- Checked the dataset for missing values and inconsistencies.
- Checked date formats in the Order Date column.
- Standardized the date display format to DD-MM-YYYY.
- Created a separate Year column using the YEAR formula.
- Used Excel formulas and Pivot Tables for sales and profit analysis.
- Organized the dataset for dashboard creation.

Formula used to extract Year:
"=YEAR(F2)"
Cancelled and Returned Orders: Their treatment could not be confirmed from the available dataset screenshots. This should be checked against the original dataset before submission.

4. KPI Definitions
1. Total Sales
Total revenue generated from all sales transactions.
Formula: "=SUM(Sales Range)"
2. Total Profit
Total profit earned from sales transactions.
Formula: "=SUM(Profit Range)"
3. Total Orders
Number of sales records/orders.
Dashboard displayed value: 9,994.

4. Profit Margin %
Percentage of sales revenue retained as profit.
Formula: "(Total Profit / Total Sales) * 100"
Note: The dashboard displays 39%, but the displayed Total Sales and Total Profit figures imply approximately 25.05%. The KPI formula and source data should be checked before final submission.

6. Key Findings
1. Overall Sales: The dashboard reports total sales of ₹1,49,56,982, indicating substantial sales activity across the business.
2. Sales Growth: The sales trend chart shows an increase from approximately ₹2.98 million in 2015 to ₹4.98 million in 2018.
3. Profitability: Total profit is displayed as ₹37,47,121.20, showing positive overall profitability.
4. Regional Performance: The dashboard compares sales performance across different regions to identify stronger and weaker markets.
5. Discount Analysis: The discount and profit relationship chart helps examine how discount levels relate to profitability.

7. Recommendations
1. Focus on high-performing product categories and regions to improve overall sales performance.
2. Monitor discount levels carefully to maintain profitability while encouraging more customer purchases.
3. Identify cities with high sales but comparatively low profit and investigate ways to improve their profit performance.
4. Use yearly sales trends to plan inventory, promotions, and business strategies.

8. How to Use the Dashboard

The dashboard provides interactive Pivot Charts and filters.

Users can:
- Filter sales by Year.
- Compare different product categories.
- Analyze regional sales performance.
- Explore city and state performance.
- Compare sales and profit.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.

Users can select available Pivot Table filters and slicers to explore different business insights.

9. Limitations
- The analysis is limited to the available dataset.
- Customer demographics and customer-level purchasing behaviour are not covered in the available dashboard.
- The dashboard does not establish the causes of changes in sales or profit.
- Returns and cancelled orders require further verification.
- The analysis is historical and does not predict future sales.
